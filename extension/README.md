# StickyInc Clipper

Right-click any page, link or selection → **Stack on StickyInc** → Read, Reply, Review or Decide. The page becomes a task in StickyInc about what you mean to do with it, like *Reply to “Q3 budget thread”*. The address and anything you'd selected are kept with it. Reply is due tomorrow at 9am; the others have no date until you give them one.

## Install (Chrome or Edge)

Until it's in the Chrome Web Store:

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`) and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this `extension` folder.
3. In StickyInc, click the gear at the bottom of the pane → **Browser clipper** → **Copy pairing code**.
4. The extension's options page opens the first time you use it (or click its toolbar icon). Paste the code, then click **Save and test**.

## Privacy

The extension only talks to StickyInc on your own computer (`127.0.0.1:47827`), and only with that pairing code, so web pages can't add tasks. It sends the page's address, its title, any text you'd selected, and your browser's name. Nothing goes anywhere else.
